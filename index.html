1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Gestion École ("the Application", "we", "us") treats user data and institutional records. We respect the privacy of educational institutions, staff, teachers, and students.

2. Data Storage & Processing (Offline & Local)

  • Local Data Storage: All student records, staff information, marks, financial entries, and timetables are stored solely in your local database on your computer or local network.
  • No Cloud Transfer: The Application does not transmit, collect, or sell personal student or staff information to external servers or third parties.
  • Licensing: The Application may only contact our licensing server to verify license key activation and validate device identifiers (Hardware ID).

3. Ownership & Data Control

The educational establishment retains full, unrestricted ownership and legal rights over all data entered into the Application. You can backup, export, or erase your database at any time without external dependency.

4. Security

We build our application following Windows security standards to safeguard your administrative files. Users are advised to protect database access using strong credentials and role privileges.
